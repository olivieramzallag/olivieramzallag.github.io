Privacy policy: gws-cli

Last updated: October 26, 2026

gws-cli is a personal tool used only by its owner to access their own Google account.

  • What it accesses: the Google Workspace data of the signed-in account (for example Gmail messages, Drive files and calendar events), only when the owner runs a command.
  • Where data goes: data is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Storage: OAuth credentials are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. Signing out with gws auth logout or revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions removes access.
  • Google API data: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
